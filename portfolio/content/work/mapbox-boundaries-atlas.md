## The goal

As Mapbox's first customer-facing engineer, I kept hitting the same two blockers in enterprise deals: customers needed administrative boundary data to join their data to geography, and regulated customers refused cloud-hosted maps. Each blocker was a missing product.

## What shipped

I drove both products from zero to one as their founding product manager. Boundaries delivered a global administrative boundary data product that let customers join their business metrics to geography. Atlas packaged self-hosted Mapbox for on-premises and air-gapped infrastructure.

I led both from initial strategy through launch before handing them over to dedicated product managers; both remain active in Mapbox's portfolio.

## What I learned

The highest-signal product insights come from stalled deals. Turning field friction into durable products is the pattern I've reused at every scale since.
