I lead software, product, and solutions engineering teams that turn emerging capabilities, especially AI agents, into reliable production workflows. Over 15+ years at Google, Instabase, Mapbox, and Caterpillar, I've built 0-to-1 products and customer-facing engineering teams across manufacturing, financial services and insurance, logistics, and the public sector.

## Current focus

**Head of DevRel, Google Maps Platform** (2022 to present)

- I lead engineering and cross-functional teams building applied AI systems, grounded MCP services, and evaluation harnesses that move builders and enterprise teams from prototype to production.
- I set the strategy and technical direction for [Code Assist](https://developers.google.com/maps/ai/code-assist), [agent skills](https://github.com/googlemaps/agent-skills), task-based agentic evals, an AI-driven Voice of Developer program, and the [Geo Architecture Center](https://developers.google.com/maps/architecture).

## Earlier roles

- **Manufacturing Industry Solutions Manager, Google Cloud** (2021 to 2022). I led the product and engineering team for [Intelligent Product Essentials](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/data-analytics/introducing-intelligent-products-essentials-for-manufacturers), combining edge AI and cloud analytics from zero to launch with GE Appliances in nine months.
- **Director, Solutions Architecture, Instabase** (2020 to 2021). I built and led the solutions architecture team landing applied AI workflows for unstructured document automation across financial services, insurance, and public-sector customers.
- **Senior Manager and Senior Solutions Architect, Mapbox** (2016 to 2020). I joined as the first customer-facing engineer, grew customer engineering from 1 to 15, and took [Boundaries](https://www.mapbox.com/boundaries) and [Atlas](https://www.mapbox.com/atlas) from zero to one for logistics, enterprise, and air-gapped public-sector deployments.
- **Technical Solutions Engineer, Caterpillar** (2012 to 2016). I built a cloud, IoT, mobile, and analytics worksite productivity system deployed across 50+ job sites and co-invented three US patents.
